Questions

Before you book

When do I have to pay?

Only after we confirm your seats. Nothing is charged when you submit the booking form.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Cancel more than 7 days before departure for a full refund; 3-7 days gets 50% back. Inside 3 days the advance is not refundable.

Are children allowed?

Children above 5 travel at the child rate. Below 5 they travel free if they share a seat with a parent.

What is included in the price?

Transport, stay, and the local guide are included on every package. Meals and entry tickets are listed per trip.

Do you run private or group trips?

Both. Group departures are on the trips page; for a private trip tell us your dates and we will quote you.

Need a custom trip?

Tell us your dates, budget and preferences — we will build a plan for you, free of charge.
